About

Hi, I'm Myesha. Thanks for stopping by. Come on in!

Before anything, I was a social worker — partnering with people who'd lost everything and working alongside them as they found their way back to themselves. There's a lot of shoveling the path and then stepping aside that comes with that work, and I'm honored to understand what leadership and building something together actually means: relentlessly supporting those around you to be the best versions of themselves.

That grounding has helped me build strong teams and stronger narratives. I'm a diversely experienced professional with a deep background in brand development, design strategy, handsome dog care, and the occasional cake bake off. But the thread that draws everything together is years of experience in human stories, and a clear sense of what the work is actually for.

I see the future of AI moving responsibly forward, supporting the wellbeing of humanity, handsome dogs, and the machines themselves. I look forward to partnering in the narratives that make that possible.
